Our Story

Professional Tin Packaging Excellence

Shanghai Youfuli Crafts & Gifts Co., Ltd. (UFREEPACK) is a premium custom tin box manufacturer based in Shanghai, China. Established in 2025, we combine a professional team with a 20,000m² Huainan production facility to serve global brands.

Our 20,000m² Huainan production facility houses 60+ servo stamping machines and 10 automated production lines, producing over 3 million tin cans monthly. With 3000+ existing molds, we serve clients in 50+ countries worldwide.

We specialize in custom tin box design, R&D, and manufacturing — from food tins and gift boxes to promotional packaging and specialty containers. Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation sets us apart.

"From a single mold to 3000+, from one workshop to 50+ countries — every tin tells a story of craftsmanship."
