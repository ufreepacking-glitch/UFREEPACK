Why UFREEPACK · Core Advantages

Built for Global B2B Buyers & Big-Account Audits

Six core pillars that make UFREEPACK the reliable packaging partner for 200+ buyers across 50+ countries.

01 · END-TO-END

One-Stop OEM / ODM Service

From artwork to finished tins — structural design, 3D render, tooling, printing, stamping, assembly, inspection and global delivery under one roof.

  • Free structural design & 3D render service
  • In-house tooling: 7–15 day new mold cycle
  • Dedicated project manager per client
02 · MOLD LIBRARY

3,000+ Free-Use Molds

Round, square, heart, hex, book, ball, house, bottle, animal — skip NRE and tap into 3,000+ ready molds across 76 sub-categories.

  • 40 industries · 23 shapes · 8 structures · 5 holidays
  • Low MOQ 5,000 pcs on in-stock molds
  • Mix-and-match lids & paper-pulp inserts
03 · FOOD SAFETY

Food-Grade Safety First

BA / MA / lacquer interior liners, FDA food-grade tests per GB 4806.9, ISO 9001, SGS material & heavy-metal reports per shipment.

  • FDA · LFGB · RoHS compliant tinplate
  • Full traceability from coil to carton
  • Non-toxic inks & food-grade varnishes
04 · PRINT & FINISH

Premium Print & Finishes

CMYK offset + Pantone spot, gold/silver hot-stamping, emboss/deboss, spot UV, matte lamination, soft-touch, metallic ink & more.

  • Six-color Heidelberg presses in-house
  • Proof-to-press color matching △E ≤ 2
  • Gold-foil registered embossing available
05 · PRODUCTION

Scalable Stamping Lines

60+ servo presses across 10 automated lines. Blanking → drawing → forming in sequence; robotic-arm transfer ensures high-volume stable quality.

  • 16T–200T press capacity on demand
  • Robotic-arm transfer ±0.03mm tolerance
  • 3 shifts · 7-day flexible production
06 · QC & LOGISTICS

Four-Stage QC + Global Logistics

AQL 2.5 full inspection before packing, 3rd-party audit welcome. FOB / CIF / DDP, EXW Shanghai/Ningbo, Amazon FBA prep & label services.

  • IQC · IPQC · FQC · OQC four-stage QC
  • Sea / Air / Rail multimodal shipping
  • US & EU warehouse drop-shipping
