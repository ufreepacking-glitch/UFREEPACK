Raw Material

Premium SPTE/TFS tinplate coil — sourced from certified mills, 100% recyclable, food-grade compliant.

Tinplate & Print

Coil cut to sheets, decorated via soy-based inks and UV cure — low-VOC, FDA-compliant finishing.

Stamping

Servo-driven presses blank, draw and form the shell — energy use cut by 30%, scrap tinplate 100% recovered.

QC & Pack

Full inspection for dimension, seal and print, then nested into recyclable cartons — zero defect tolerance.

Ship

Loaded for global dispatch — at end-of-life the empty tin returns to the mill, closing the loop.
